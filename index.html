FAQs

Common questions.

Do I need a skip permit?

No. Unlike a traditional skip on a public road, our trailers are placed on your driveway or private land, meaning no council permit is required. Most customers don't need one at all.

Are you a licensed waste carrier?

Yes. SkipIt holds an Upper Tier Waste Carrier licence, legally authorising us to collect, transport and dispose of controlled waste. We can provide our licence number on request.

How quickly can you deliver?

We typically deliver next working day for bookings made before noon. Same-day delivery may be available depending on your location — WhatsApp us to check.

What if I need the trailer longer?

No problem. Let us know at booking, or message us before collection day and we'll extend your hire. Additional days are charged at the same daily rate.

What areas do you cover?

We cover Greater Belfast including Lisburn, Newtownabbey, Castlereagh, North Down and Antrim. Not sure if we reach you? Give us a shout — we'll let you know.

What happens to the waste?

Everything goes to a licensed recycling and waste management facility. We aim to recover and recycle as much material as possible, and we can provide waste transfer notes for compliance purposes.
