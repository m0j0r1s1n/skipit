Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

SkipIt provides trailer hire, dump runs, house clearances and property cleaning in Bangor and North Down. You can contact us about your data at hello@skipit.work.

What we collect

When you submit a booking or quote form we collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, dates, type of waste, trailer or service choice and any details you add.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, provide a quote, arrange delivery and collection of a trailer, and meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Form submissions are stored in a database hosted on Cloudflare. If our system cannot save a submission, the form content may be sent to our form provider, Formspree, so that we do not lose your enquiry. We do not sell your information.

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics only if you choose Accept on the cookie notice. If you choose Reject, no analytics cookies are set. You can change your choice below.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and quote information for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it. Contact us at hello@skipit.work. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
